How to judge these

  • Squint test: at the 16px step, can you still tell whose logo it is?
  • Story test: would a DON get the idea without the caption?
  • Company test: does it look at home next to the Fraunces wordmark, on ivory and on ink?
  • Ownable test: could a competitor have shipped the same mark? (C is weakest here, B strongest.)

All four are hand-drawn SVG — infinitely scalable, two colors max, no gradients. Whichever wins gets a refinement pass (optical corrections, outlined wordmark, favicon/OG/app-icon exports, nav integration).
