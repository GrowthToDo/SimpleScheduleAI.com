### Drafted rewrites — AWAITING FOUNDER REVIEW, nothing shipped

Scope: title tag and meta description only. No body claims touched.

**R1. `/blog/shiftwizard-vs-managed-service`** (400 impressions across four
queries, positions 6.7–8.7, 0 clicks). The searcher is asking a capability
question; the title answers with a vendor comparison format.

- Title now: `What Is ShiftWizard? A Review vs a Managed Scheduling Service (2026)`
- Title proposed: `What Is ShiftWizard? Overtime, Certifications and Self-Service Reviewed (2026)`
- Rationale: the three highest-volume queries all name overtime, certification
  compliance or mobile self-service. Putting those nouns in the title matches the
  question that was typed. `What Is ShiftWizard` stays first because it is itself
  a 69-impression query at position 8.7.

**R2. `/blog/shiftwizard-alternatives`** — resolve the split by narrowing, not by
consolidating. The page outranks its sibling on both shared queries but is being
shown for review-and-capability questions it is not written to answer.

- Title now: `4 Best ShiftWizard Alternatives for Critical Access Hospitals (2026)`
- Title proposed: unchanged.
- Meta proposed: lead with "switching from ShiftWizard" rather than with what
  ShiftWizard does, so Google has a cleaner signal that this page serves
  replacement intent and the sibling serves evaluation intent. Exact wording to
  be drafted once the founder confirms the split-by-intent approach over a 301.

**R3. `/blog/aladtec-alternatives`** (55 impressions at 7.5 on `aladtec star ems`,
0 clicks; also carried 20 impressions at position 2.5 on `best aladtec
alternatives` with 0 clicks in the July queue). Aladtec rewrote its own homepage
this month to name fire, EMS and law enforcement explicitly. `aladtec star ems` is
an EMS query, and we are showing up for it with a critical-access-hospital page.

- Recommendation: **do not rewrite to chase it.** It is out-of-ICP traffic and
  winning it would pull the page away from the buyer we want. Logged so it stops
  reappearing in the queue each month.
