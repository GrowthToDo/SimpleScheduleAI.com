### Finding: ShiftWizard cannibalization (539 impressions split)

Two live posts rank for the SAME two queries. Google alternates which one it
picks: `alternatives` wins one (4.5 vs 8.1), `vs-managed-service` wins the other
(7.0 vs 6.5). Neither consolidates, so both sit mid-page-1 instead of one owning it.

### Finding: template terms are small, and belong to the resources page

competition for the smaller term while diluting the larger one.
