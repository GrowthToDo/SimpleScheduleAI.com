**Hook (illustrative grid, not a rule):**

- 2 licensed nurses on every 12-hour shift is 28 nurse-shifts, or 336 hours, a week.
- A nurse on three 12s works 36 hours (0.9 FTE per Hunt, L226), so 9.3 nurses before time off.
- With time off, the relief-factor post's 9.6 FTEs / 0.9 is about 10.6 nurses.
- 14 charge slots a week means at least 5 charge-qualified nurses.
- A 4-week cycle has 112 shifts to fill.
- Matches that post (T10).
