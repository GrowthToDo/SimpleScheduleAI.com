## The angle

Two things meet on January 1, 2027. Texas's Medicaid application backlog delays decisions, and
P.L. 119-21 section 71112 cuts how far back Medicaid pays from three months before the
application month to two. The window counts from the month the application is MADE, not the
month Texas processes it, so the backlog delays payment while the filing month decides whether a
stay is covered at all. Hook: the transition example (an October 2026 stay is covered if the
application is filed in December 2026 and not if it is filed in January 2027).

## Sources read at source (2026-11-17)

- P.L. 119-21 sec. 71112, govinfo PLAW-119publ21 (curl): new 1902(a)(34)(B) "in or after the
  second month before the month in which the individual made application"; struck 1905(a) text
  "in or after the third month before the month in which the recipient makes application";
  effective for applications "made on or after the first day of the first quarter that begins
  after December 31, 2026"; coverage only "if such individual was (or upon application would
  have been) eligible for such assistance at the time such care and services were furnished".
- 1 TAC 366.325 (Cornell LII, curl), Texas pregnant women's Medicaid: "Retroactive coverage may
  begin as early as three months before the application month." Amended eff. 6/2/2024.

  backlog "from about 1,600 in January to more than 210,000 in August"; processing within 45
