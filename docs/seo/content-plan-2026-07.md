**Recommendation**: don't fight big players for the generic head term (low ROI for a CAH-niche brand). ## Playbook: closing the Perplexity / Copilot gap

**Why we lose them while we win Google AIO + ChatGPT:** Perplexity and Copilot ground answers on **third-party validation** — directories (Capterra, G2, GetApp, Software Advice, TrustRadius, f6s), third-party "best X" roundups, and **Reddit** — more than on a vendor's own site. Copilot grounds on the Bing index, but even where we rank #1-2 organic its *answer* picked competitors: organic rank ≠ citation. This is ~70% off-page, ~30% content.

**Levers, in priority order:**

1. **Get listed on the directories these engines cite (highest ROI, off-content).** Claim/create profiles on Capterra, G2, GetApp, Software Advice, TrustRadius, Crunchbase, f6s — in the "Nurse Scheduling Software" / "Healthcare Scheduling" categories. Even pre-launch, a claimed listing with the CAH-Texas positioning makes us retrievable. **A few genuine early reviews** move us out of "0 reviews" (which ChatGPT flagged as a caveat) into the consideration set.
2. **Get MENTIONED in third-party roundups**, not just our own. The engines cite other sites' "best nurse scheduling software for small hospitals" listicles. Pitch SSAI as the CAH-specific option to the writers/sites already ranking for that term (direct outreach, HARO/Qwoted/Featured). One mid-authority mention can flip Perplexity.
3. **Reddit / community (authentic, disclosed).** Perplexity treats Reddit as first-class. Pradeep genuinely answering scheduling-software questions in r/nursing, r/nursemanagers with disclosure ("I build SimpleScheduleAI; honest take…") is the right, non-spammy path. Astroturf backfires. Slow but durable.
4. **Entity/knowledge consistency.** Consistent "SimpleScheduleAI = AI-native nurse scheduling service for Texas CAHs" across LinkedIn, Crunchbase, directory profiles, and schema (Organization + SoftwareApplication) so an LLM confidently names us. Directly closes the Perplexity mis-attribution ("managed service for CAHs" → schedulingwiz.com).
5. **Answer-extractable content (support).** Clean, entity-first, self-contained passages on our pages ("SimpleScheduleAI is an AI-native nurse scheduling service for Texas Critical Access Hospitals that…"). The "Is AI scheduling safe?" + comparison content in the plan targets the exact concepts these engines surface.
6. **Monitor monthly.** Re-probe Perplexity + Copilot with the head queries (the llm-citation-log workflow); track whether listings/mentions move the needle; kill what doesn't.

**Bottom line:** Levers 1-2 (directories + third-party roundups) are the highest-ROI and are founder/outreach actions, not content. Content alone won't flip these two engines.
