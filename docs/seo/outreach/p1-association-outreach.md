Created 2026-08-02. This is the top priority lane per the founder's "focus on

## Guardrails binding every email below

- **Service mode.** Self-label "AI-native nurse scheduling service". If pricing
  ever comes up: flat $1,000/month up to 20 nurses, $1,500/month for 21 to 40,
  no per-nurse fees, no setup fees. NEVER the $10/user product figure here.
- **Disclose the vendor relationship up front.** These are curated public-service
  resource lists. Hiding that we are a vendor would be both dishonest and
  counterproductive; RHIhub's submission already carried the disclosure.
- **No pilot, customer, cohort, or outcome claims.** SimpleScheduleAI is
  pre-launch with zero customers.
- **Ask for nothing in return.** Both assets are genuinely free and ungated. No
  reciprocal link request, ever (Google link-scheme policy, and it reads badly to
  an association).
- **Statistics only from `../facts-dossier.md`** with their primary sources and
  the correct year attribution.
- No em-dashes or en-dashes. Keep every email under ~200 words; association staff
  are busy and short emails get read.
